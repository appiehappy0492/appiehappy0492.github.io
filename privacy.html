Privacy policy — Pulsar

Last updated: 13 November 2026 · Developer: AppieHappyGames · Contact: appiehappygames@gmail.com

Pulsar is an offline arcade game. This policy explains what data is involved when you play.

Data the game stores

Your progress (scores, stars, Stardust, unlocked items and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers of our own, we have no user accounts, and we do not receive this data. The only exception is the optional play statistics described below, which are off unless you turn them on. Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your device.

Play statistics (optional)

The game asks once whether you want to share anonymous play statistics. Nothing is collected or sent before you say yes. If you say no, or close the question, nothing is ever sent.

If you say yes, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase (provided by Google) to count how the game is played, so we can see which systems are too hard or too easy. This is what is collected:

  • a random app-instance ID (not your name, not your account, not your advertising ID),
  • play events, such as which systems are won or lost, how many waves you reached, the game mode, and how long a run took (no free text, nothing you type),
  • your device type and Android version,
  • your country, based on a truncated IP address, and
  • in-app purchase events from Google Play (that a purchase happened, not your payment details).

The advertising ID is not collected by Analytics, and the data is not used for advertising or ad personalization. You can switch this off at any time under Settings → Privacy → Share play statistics. Switching it off stops any further collection. Data already collected is anonymous and is kept by Google under its own retention rules. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one (for example for a second chance). These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Pulsar Plus" purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only learn whether the purchase is active on your account.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games, your scores and Play Games profile are processed by Google to show leaderboards. You can manage this in the Google Play Games app.

Children

Pulsar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data ourselves, and the optional play statistics are anonymous, so we cannot look up or delete data about one person; you can contact us with any question. For data processed by Google, use Google's privacy tools. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
