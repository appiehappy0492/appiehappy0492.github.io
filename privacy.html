Privacy policy — Pulsar

Last updated: 16 November 2026 · Developer: AppieHappyGames · Contact: appiehappygames@gmail.com

Pulsar is an arcade game that works offline. This policy explains what data is involved when you play.

Data the game stores

Your progress (scores, stars, Stardust, unlocked items and settings) is saved only on your device. We do not run servers of our own, we have no user accounts, and we do not receive this data. The only exception is the optional play statistics described below, which are off unless you turn them on. Uninstalling the game deletes the data on your device.

Play statistics (optional)

The game asks once whether you want to share anonymous play statistics. Nothing is collected or sent before you say yes. If you say no, or close the question, nothing is ever sent.

If you say yes, the game uses Google Analytics for Firebase (provided by Google) to count how the game is played, so we can see which systems are too hard or too easy. This is what is collected:

  • a random app-instance ID (not your name, not your account, not your advertising ID),
  • play events, such as which systems are won or lost, how many waves you reached, the game mode, and how long a run took (no free text, nothing you type). The complete list of event names: run_start, run_end, system_won, tutorial_done, mode_unlocked, daily_played, reward_ad_shown, plus_viewed; plus two labels: channel (test or store build) and cohort (your answer to "How did you find Pulsar?", only asked in the test build),
  • your device type and Android version,
  • your country, based on a truncated IP address, and
  • in-app purchase events from Google Play (that a purchase happened, not your payment details).

The advertising ID is not collected by Analytics, and the data is not used for advertising or ad personalization. You can switch this off at any time under Settings → Privacy → Share play statistics. Switching it off stops any further collection. Data already collected is anonymous and is kept by Google under its own retention rules. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy.

Daily reminder (optional)

After you have played a Daily Challenge, the game asks once whether you would like a reminder. If you say yes, Android shows its own permission window for notifications. If you say yes to both, the game can show one local notification per day about the Daily Challenge, and none on a day you already played it. The notification is planned on your device by Android; nothing is sent to a server, and the game collects no data for it. If you say no, it is never asked again by itself. You can switch it off at any time under Settings → Daily reminder, and in the Android settings for notifications.

Challenge codes

After a run you can share a short challenge code. Challenge codes contain only a game seed, mode and score. They carry no name, no ID and nothing about your device. The game never sends a code anywhere: you share it yourself, as text, through your own messaging app, and a friend types it in. We do not receive it.

Game events (one small download)

When your device is online, the game downloads one small file with game events (for example a weekend with extra Stardust) from GitHub Pages (appiehappy0492.github.io). The request is a plain download: the game sends no information about you or your progress with it, and it works the same without it. GitHub, which hosts the file, sees your IP address when it is requested, as with any web page; see GitHub's privacy statement. We do not receive anything from this request.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The game can show rewarded video ads, only when you choose to watch one: a second chance, a retry from a boss checkpoint (after the free one), a fourth upgrade card, new upgrade cards, bonus Stardust or Stardust in the shop. Now and then, when you leave the result screen after a game over (a lost Galaxy system or the end of an Endless, Daily or other run), the game can also show a short full-screen ad (an interstitial) that you can close: never in your first session, not in your first 3 runs of a day, at most one every 3 minutes, never after a won system, never during play and never right after a rewarded ad. With Pulsar Plus the game shows no ads at all and loads none. These ads are provided by Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and process data such as your device's advertising ID, IP address (approximate location), and information about ad interactions, to show and measure ads and to prevent fraud. In the EEA, UK and Switzerland you are asked for consent first; you can change your choice at any time under Settings → Privacy choices. Google's privacy policy: policies.google.com/privacy and how Google uses data from partner apps: policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings.

Purchases (Google Play)

The optional "Pulsar Plus" purchase is handled by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details; we only learn whether the purchase is active on your account.

Leaderboards (Google Play Games)

If you use Google Play Games, your scores and Play Games profile are processed by Google to show leaderboards. You can manage this in the Google Play Games app.

Children

Pulsar is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Your rights

We do not hold personal data ourselves, and the optional play statistics are anonymous, so we cannot look up or delete data about one person; you can contact us with any question. For data processed by Google, use Google's privacy tools. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority (in the Netherlands: Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens).

Changes
